How the pipeline works

Exact search aligns the query to every sequence. The prefilter, adapted from BLAST and MMseqs2, finds cheap evidence of similarity first and runs the expensive exact alignment only on sequences that show it.

1. Index the databaseEvery 3-mer, with the sequence and position where it occurs. Built once.
2. SeedLook up the query's 3-mers, or every similar word scoring at least T.
3. Two hitsKeep sequences with at least 2 seeds on the same diagonal, as in BLAST 2.0.
4. Ungapped scoreBest ungapped score on that diagonal must reach S (MMseqs2 stage 2).
5. Exact alignmentVectorized Smith-Waterman (A2) on the survivors.

A1 and A2: same maths, fewer Python steps

A1 (SWIPE, Rognes 2011) fills each DP cell for a batch of database sequences in one vector operation, which is 14× faster. A2 (scan-based SW, Khajeh-Saeed et al. 2010) removes the left-to-right dependency on horizontal gaps. That dependency becomes one cumulative maximum, so each query row is a handful of array operations. Together with int16 arithmetic and length-sorted batches of 64, A2 is 45× faster with identical scores.

Second round of profiling

Once the prefilter was added, it became the bottleneck. About 70% of its time went to diagonal scoring and 27% to rebuilding word neighborhoods for every query. Precomputing the neighbor table once and scoring in int16 made it 2.7× faster (0.093 s to 0.035 s per query at T=12) with identical candidate sets.

Limits and next steps

Synthetic benchmark. 40 protein families were evolved at 12 divergence levels using BLOSUM62-derived substitution probabilities plus indels, alongside 520 unrelated decoys. This gives exact ground truth, but real proteins have low-complexity regions and skewed composition. The next step is SCOPe/ASTRAL, the standard homology benchmark.

No E-values. A hit counts if it outscores every unrelated sequence for that query, the "sensitivity up to the first false positive".

NumPy, not SIMD C. Absolute times are far from SWIPE or MMseqs2. The relative findings are the point. A good extension would be to benchmark against Parasail.
