Protein Search Explorer

Put in a protein, search it against the 1,000-protein test database with Smith-Waterman, and look inside any comparison. Everything runs in your browser.

The search compares your protein with each of the 1,000 database proteins and gives each one a similarity score. Higher means more alike. Click any result to look inside that comparison.

Inside one comparison

This is the grid Smith-Waterman fills in. Your protein runs down the side and the database protein across the top. Each square holds the best score of any alignment ending there, and darker blue means higher. The red line is the best alignment, traced back from the highest square.

Top line: your protein. Bottom: the database protein. | marks an identical letter, + a similar one, and - a gap.

The 1,000-protein test database


These proteins were generated by make_benchmark.py. 480 are relatives of the 40 test proteins, evolved with mutations so that they share 15% to 90% of their letters. The other 520 are random decoys related to nothing. Click a row to search with that protein.
